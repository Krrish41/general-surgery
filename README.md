## 15-Mark University Examination Structure

Every surgical topic in this monograph strictly adheres to the university 15-mark essay blueprint:
1. **Definition, Classification & Epidemiology**
2. **Surgical Anatomy & Applied Embryology** (landmarks, fascial planes, triangles)
3. **Etiopathogenesis & Pathophysiology** (cellular mechanisms, bacteriology)
4. **Clinical Presentation** (Inspection, Palpation, Percussion, Auscultation, Named Signs)
5. **Structured Differential Diagnosis** (comprehensive comparative matrices)
6. **Diagnostic Workup** (Laboratory, Ultrasound, CT/MRI, FNAC/Biopsy, Staging)
7. **Comprehensive Management**:
   - Resuscitation & Preoperative preparation
   - Medical & Conservative therapy
   - Detailed Surgical Technique (Incision, Exposure, Steps, Structures to preserve, Closure)
   - Postoperative monitoring & complication management
8. **Prognosis & Follow-up**
9. **High-Yield Examiner Viva Pearls**
10. **Must-Draw Schematics & Diagrams**

## Complete Syllabus Coverage (24 Chapters)

### Part I: Core General Surgery Principles
- **Chapter 1**: Shock & Surgical Fluid Resuscitation *(Hemodynamics, Pathophysiology, Fluid Protocols)*
- **Chapter 2**: Blood Transfusion & Transfusion Reactions *(PRBCs, FFP, Platelets, TRALI vs TACO, Complications)*
- **Chapter 3**: Surgical Infections *(Cellulitis, Erysipelas, Boils, Carbuncle, Necrotizing Fasciitis)*
- **Chapter 4**: Acute Abscess & Hilton's Method of Incision and Drainage *(Cardinal signs, Technique)*
- **Chapter 5**: Surgical Site Infections (SSI) & Prophylactic Antibiotics *(CDC Classification, Prevention)*
- **Chapter 6**: Gas Gangrene, Tetanus & Severe Soft Tissue Infections *(Clostridial myonecrosis, Antitoxin)*

### Part II: Head, Neck, Diathermy & Maxillofacial Trauma
- **Chapter 7**: Swellings of the Neck *(Thyroglossal Cyst, Branchial Cyst, Cystic Hygroma, Carotid Body Tumor, 10-Swelling Matrix)*
- **Chapter 8**: Cervical Lymphadenopathy *(Tubercular Cold Abscess, Collar-stud abscess, Metastatic nodes)*
- **Chapter 9**: Wound Healing, Scars & Wound Care *(Phases of repair, Hypertrophic scar vs Keloid, VAC)*
- **Chapter 10**: Surgical Diathermy & Electrosurgery *(Monopolar vs Bipolar, Grounding, Complications)*
- **Chapter 11**: Maxillofacial & Mandibular Trauma *(Mandible fractures, Le Fort I, II, III fractures, Fixation)*

### Part III: Surgical Infections, Neurosurgery & Operating Theater Setup
- **Chapter 12**: Abdominal Tuberculosis *(Ulcerative, Hyperplastic, Peritoneal forms, Pulled-up cecum)*
- **Chapter 13**: Actinomycosis *(Actinomyces israelii, Sulfur granules, Wooden induration)*
- **Chapter 14**: Trigeminal Neuralgia & Facial Pain *(Tic Douloureux, Medical therapy, Jannetta decompression)*
- **Chapter 15**: Head Injury & Intracranial Hemorrhages *(Extradural Hematoma vs Subdural Hematoma, GCS)*
- **Chapter 16**: Sterilization, Disinfection & Theater Setup *(Autoclaving, Ethylene Oxide, Cidex, Zoning)*
- **Chapter 17**: Surgical Sutures & Needles *(Absorbable vs Non-absorbable, Natural vs Synthetic, Suture sizes)*
- **Chapter 18**: Surgical Drains & Tubes *(Open vs Closed suction, Rationale, Removal criteria)*

### Part IV: Advanced Technologies, Anesthesia & Thyroid Gland
- **Chapter 19**: Lasers in Modern Surgery *(CO2, Nd:YAG, Holmium, Laser safety principles)*
- **Chapter 20**: Cryosurgery *(Liquid nitrogen, Intracellular crystallization, Indications)*
- **Chapter 21**: Principles of Surgical Anesthesia *(General, Spinal, Epidural, Local infiltration, Sellick maneuver)*
- **Chapter 22**: Skin Grafts & Local Flaps *(STSG vs FTSG, Donor site care, Graft failure mechanisms)*
- **Chapter 23**: Thyroid Gland: Surgical Anatomy, Neoplasms & Thyroidectomy *(Papillary, Follicular, Medullary, Anaplastic, Steps of Thyroidectomy, RLN preservation, Thyroid storm)*

### Part V: Rapid Revision
- **Chapter 24**: Master Surgery Rapid Revision Cheat Sheet *(High-yield matrices, formulas, scores, and exam night pearls)*
